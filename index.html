Nashville Near Me

What's happening near me?

Type a Nashville address to see the rezonings, zoning appeals and building permits around it. Everything comes straight from Metro Nashville's public records, with each record's case or permit number so you can look it up.

Where this comes from

  • Zoning and planning cases: Metro Planning's Development Tracker. Cases still open are shown, with the Planning Commission hearing date when one is set. If a case became a council bill, it links to the bill's plain-language page on the Nashville Council Digest.
  • Zoning appeals: Board of Zoning Appeals cases heard in the last year or scheduled.
  • Building permits: permits issued in the last year, and applications still in review, from Metro Codes.
  • Your address is matched against Metro's parcel map. Distances are measured from that parcel.

The descriptions are Metro's own words. Nothing here is written by AI; phone numbers and email addresses in permit notes are removed, and owner and applicant names are never requested. Not affiliated with Metro Nashville; always confirm with the official record. How this works.
